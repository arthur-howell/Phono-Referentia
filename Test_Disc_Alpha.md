# PHONO REFERENTIA

### *Cartridge Testing for Phono Education*

**Reference Disc I — Dynamics & Vocals**  
**2026**

---

## Project Overview

**Phono Referentia** is an ongoing phono-cartridge reference, testing, documentation, and educational project created to support controlled comparisons of phono cartridges and styli using repeatable musical source material.

The project was developed in conjunction with a **commercial, for-profit YouTube channel** focused on phono cartridges, styli, turntables, analog playback equipment, and related audio technology.

Phono Referentia provides the controlled reference material used in those evaluations.

Rather than relying upon commercially released recordings owned by third parties, the project uses original musical material created specifically for Phono Referentia. This allows the reference material to be designed around the characteristics being evaluated while also maintaining the original digital masters as documented reference sources.

The fundamental methodology is simple:

> **Create a known digital reference, transfer that reference to a physical record, and use the same physical record when evaluating different cartridges and styli.**

The resulting recordings, measurements, documentation, and observations can then be used in the production of reviews, demonstrations, educational material, and other content produced for the associated YouTube channel.

Its primary emphasis is **dynamic reproduction and vocal performance**, while also incorporating material useful for evaluating tracking, transient response, stereo separation, frequency reproduction, musical complexity, and other characteristics of phono playback.

The disc contains complete musical compositions rather than conventional laboratory test tones.

This is intentional.

The objective of Phono Referentia is to evaluate cartridges under conditions representative of actual music reproduction while retaining substantially greater control over the source material than would be possible using unrelated commercial recordings.

# Musical Source Material

All musical works contained on **Phono Referentia Reference Disc I** were created specifically for this project using an internally controlled AI-assisted music-generation process operated by the project creator.

The compositions were generated, selected, assembled, sequenced, and prepared specifically for their use as phono reference material.

The resulting individual recordings were assembled into complete Side A and Side B digital masters.

These side masters establish the originating digital references for Reference Disc I.

They are preserved separately from all subsequent recordings made from the physical vinyl record.

## Side B — Tracking & Complexity Reference

**Total Duration: Approximately 17 minutes**

1. **Stand Your Ground**
2. **Velvet Nights — Lounge N House Remix**
3. **We Were Over in the Little**

Side B places greater emphasis on **complexity, high-frequency reproduction, stereo separation, and tracking performance**.

The arrangements incorporate combinations of percussion, cymbals, guitars, keyboards, electronic instrumentation, vocal harmonies, and increasingly dense musical information.

The progression toward the end of the side is intentional.

Material located nearer the inner grooves is designed to place increasing demands on the playback system.

The final material includes deliberately elevated complexity and significant high-frequency information, with the most demanding portions intentionally biased toward the **left channel / inner groove wall**.

This provides a musical stress test for characteristics including:

- Inner-groove tracking
- High-frequency tracing
- Sibilance
- Cymbal reproduction
- Transient definition
- Channel-specific mistracking
- Congestion
- Separation
- Distortion under difficult groove conditions

The result remains a musical composition rather than a conventional tracking-test signal.

# Reference Methodology

The original digital masters establish the baseline for the project.

Those masters are then used to manufacture the physical **Phono Referentia Reference Disc**.

Once the physical reference record exists, cartridge evaluations are performed using that record rather than repeatedly using the digital masters.

The fundamental comparison chain is therefore:

```text
ORIGINAL DIGITAL MASTER
          ↓
    REFERENCE DISC
          ↓
  CARTRIDGE / STYLUS
          ↓
      PHONO STAGE
          ↓
          ADC
          ↓
    DIGITAL CAPTURE
```

This allows multiple cartridges to encounter the same musical program through the same physical medium.

---

# MASTER → DISC → CAPTURE

Phono Referentia divides its reference material into three primary categories.

## MASTER

The **MASTER** is the originating digital source.

For Reference Disc I, the complete Side A and Side B digital masters establish what existed before the material was transferred to vinyl.

These files are preserved as the project's primary digital reference.

## DISC

The **DISC** is the manufactured physical Phono Referentia record.

The disc introduces the characteristics inherent to cutting, manufacturing, groove geometry, vinyl playback, and the physical medium itself.

The same reference disc is used whenever practical during cartridge comparisons.

## CAPTURE

A **CAPTURE** is a digital recording made while a particular cartridge and stylus reproduce the physical reference disc.

Each capture therefore contains the cumulative result of:

```text
Digital Master
      ↓
Vinyl Transfer
      ↓
Physical Groove
      ↓
Stylus
      ↓
Cartridge Generator
      ↓
Tonearm
      ↓
Phono Stage
      ↓
ADC
      ↓
Capture
```

The relationship can therefore be summarized as:

> **MASTER → DISC → CAPTURE**

This relationship forms the core of the Phono Referentia methodology.

# Why Original Reference Music Is Used

Commercial recordings present several problems for a long-term reference project.

Different recordings have different mastering histories, licensing conditions, copyright owners, production techniques, and availability.

They also were not created specifically to investigate cartridge behavior.

Phono Referentia instead establishes its own controlled musical reference.

This provides several advantages:

1. **Known source material**
2. **Consistent physical reference**
3. **Purpose-designed musical passages**
4. **Original digital masters available for comparison**
5. **Long-term archival availability**
6. **Repeatability across cartridge evaluations**
7. **Freedom to construct tests around specific playback characteristics**
8. **Ability to preserve complete cartridge captures**
9. **A consistent reference library that can grow over time**

The objective is not to eliminate music from cartridge testing.

It is to make the music itself part of the controlled methodology.

Where practical, publicly released master and capture files should have corresponding **SHA-256 checksums**.


# Project Objective

Phono Referentia does **not** attempt to establish a universally correct cartridge or stylus.

A cartridge is part of a mechanical and electrical playback system, and different designs can exhibit different strengths, weaknesses, interactions, and subjective characteristics.

The objective is instead to create reference material that makes those differences easier to:

- **Hear**
- **Measure**
- **Compare**
- **Document**
- **Reproduce**
- **Discuss**
- **Teach**
- **Study**

The public masters provide the originating reference.

The physical record provides the common analog medium.

The cartridge captures document what different playback systems recover from that medium.

The associated videos interpret and communicate those results.
